EPIC-1: Evaluate Care v0.1.4

Capacity: 80%

Goal: Release a credible version 1 of the Evaluate Care business component. Evaluate Care contains two separate and distinct capabilities: Evaluate Care for Providers and Evaluate Care for Clinical Trials. These are independent capabilities but may share measures where applicable. Each capability must deliver a quality score derived from a minimum of 3 credible measures, with a substantive, explainable answer showing what measures produced that score and how each measure contributed. If we can deliver 3 credible measures for both providers and clinical trials, we have an alpha component. Stretch goal: allow the user to adjust the algorithm to determine quality by adjusting the importance of the different measures that are used to determine quality.

Boss's non-binding suggestion: Prescription behavior mapped to diseases. This is the secret AI-enhanced feature — the ability to analyze how a provider prescribes relative to the conditions they treat. Premium differentiator.

Claude's non-binding suggestions for measures (replace or add as you see fit):
- Provider: board certifications, patient ratings/reviews, malpractice or disciplinary history, years in practice, specialty match accuracy, acceptance of new patients
- Clinical trials: trial phase (I/II/III/IV), enrollment status, relevance to user's condition, sponsor credibility, proximity to user, recency of results

Description: Evaluate Care is one of four business components (Find Care, Evaluate Care, Discuss Care, The Brain). Find Care is stable and ships as-is for the alpha. This epic delivers the first shippable version of Evaluate Care — the system's ability to assess, score, and explain healthcare provider quality and clinical trial relevance to consumers. Evaluate Care for Providers and Evaluate Care for Clinical Trials are separate capabilities with independent feature sets, scoring, and explainability. Measures may be shared where the same data source applies. Each quality measure is a feature.

Success Criteria:
- The system can tell the user what a provider's quality is and why we say so
- The system can tell the user what a clinical trial's quality/relevance is and why we say so
- Evaluate Care for Providers and Evaluate Care for Clinical Trials are separately functional
- Every quality assessment includes a quality score derived from minimum 3 distinct measures
- Every quality score is coupled with the measures that produced it
- Quality assessments are grounded in data (not fabricated by the LLM)
- Quality signals are explainable
- No regressions from v0.1.3 Find Care baseline
- STRETCH: User can adjust the weight of individual quality measures and the score recalculates

Definition of Done: Boss signs off as sufficient subsequent to UAT.

Minimum features (define the specific measures):
- EVAL-SCORE-PROV: Provider Quality Scoring Engine (backend, Providers) — composite score from 3+ measures, deterministic
- EVAL-SCORE-CT: Clinical Trial Quality Scoring Engine (backend, Clinical Trials) — composite score from 3+ measures, deterministic
- EVAL-EXPLAIN: Score Explainability (backend, Shared) — return measures, values, contributions
- EVAL-EXPLAIN-UX: Score Explainability Display (frontend, Shared) — render in chat. Depends on EVAL-SCORE-PROV, EVAL-SCORE-CT, EVAL-EXPLAIN
- EVAL-P-MEASURE-1/2/3: At least 3 provider quality measures (backend) — define what, data source, why credible
- EVAL-P-RX: Prescription Behavior Analysis (backend, Providers) — Boss's non-binding suggestion
- EVAL-CT-MEASURE-1/2/3: At least 3 clinical trial quality measures (backend) — define what, data source, why credible
- EVAL-WEIGHTS: User-Adjustable Quality Weights (frontend+backend, Shared) — STRETCH

GPT and Claude are free to recommend a different feature set if it better serves the epic goal.
